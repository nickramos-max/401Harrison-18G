Property Overview

Elevated City Living
at The Harrison

Experience elevated city living at The Harrison in this beautifully designed one-bedroom, one-bath residence showcasing sweeping Twin Peaks and Bay water views through dramatic floor-to-ceiling windows.

Thoughtfully appointed with brand-new hardwood flooring, premium appliances, and signature Waterworks brass fixtures, this sophisticated home blends timeless luxury with modern comfort. A private balcony off the primary bedroom frames the city skyline.

Located in the heart of Rincon Hill, the residence offers convenient access to transportation, the Financial District, Downtown, the Embarcadero, the Ferry Building, world-class dining, museums, and San Francisco’s vibrant waterfront lifestyle.

The building’s interiors were designed by acclaimed San Francisco designer Ken Fulk, establishing The Harrison as a premier full-service luxury high-rise. Residents enjoy a 24-hour attended lobby and valet service, along with Uncle Harry’s — a spectacular 49th-floor residents’ lounge featuring a bar, dining salon, parlor, speakeasy, and panoramic Bay Bridge and water views. Additional amenities include a heated outdoor pool terrace with BBQ area, a fitness center, wine storage, and a signature breakfast pantry with complimentary coffee service.
